**Current verified native source:** [`3b082900184d47109895c4e4e78eae1f6fce8ce8`](https://github.com/dwijayuda/pskernel/commit/3b082900184d47109895c4e4e78eae1f6fce8ce8), strengthened regression [`b6205d34`](https://github.com/dwijayuda/pskernel/commit/b6205d34b6d4b3f44477d0bae725a1a424db1296), and outer-dependent-binder differential [`4007d03c`](https://github.com/dwijayuda/pskernel/commit/4007d03ce4a63e9ea10d51c9052fc95a348beb72). **Full native CI 38270573104 passed** Lean 4.34 build, malformed/negative and exact loose-range regression, complete native microbenchmarks, and same-run independently built unchanged-original checker: https://github.com/dwijayuda/pskernel/actions/runs/38270573104. **Root cause and design:** Both `psKernelDefEqLambdaSpineWithFuel` and `psKernelDefEqForallSpineWithFuel` previously grew oldest-first substitution lists using `psKernelExprListAppend subst [fresh]` at every binder, then reversed those growing lists again on every domain/terminal instantiation via `psKernelExprInstantiateRev`. Together these form quadratic costs in binder depth. The redesigned workers keep a **newest-first substitution stack via `List.cons`**, matching de Bruijn substitution order directly through `psKernelExprInstantiate`; no append/reverse loop remains. For matched binder prefixes of at least 12 lambdas or Pi binders, one **bottom-up bounded, exact syntactic loose-variable analysis** computes each body’s range once in `psKernelC0BinderBodyRangesWithFuel`, a unified lambda/Pi helper in `Core/Expr.lean`. At each recursion, the two body ranges decide whether the original worker would open a local; when metadata is absent, cannot complete under bounded fuel, or a shallow/nonmatching head occurs, it uses the historical exact body scan.
