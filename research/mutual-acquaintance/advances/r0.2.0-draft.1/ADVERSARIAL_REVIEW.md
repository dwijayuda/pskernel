# Adversarial internal review — research r0.2.0-draft.1

**Date:** 2026-11-19. **Reviewer:** internal critical audit, *not* an independent expert or peer review.  
**Works assessed:** [Tractatus v2.0.0-draft.2](../../editions/v2.0.0-draft.2/Tractatus_de_Perceptione_et_Amore_ID.md), [permission-relative identification](PERMISSION_RELATIVE_IDENTIFICATION.md), [sequential bound](SEQUENTIAL_BOUND.md), and [pilot blueprint](PILOT_PROTOCOL.md).

## Decision: conditional continuation, not publication

There is an intellectually credible question and a coherent philosophical manuscript. There is not yet an established novel result in philosophy, statistics, or relationship science. The next advancement must survive the following objections without merely renaming them.

| Challenge | Why a skeptical reviewer would press it | Necessary answer / current disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Novelty of relation formation** | Shared experiences influencing a relationship were experimentally studied by Aron et al. (2000) and earlier studies; Eastwick et al. (2026) explicitly propose compatibility built over time. | Do not claim discovery of co-formation; isolate a narrower distinction that yields a new empirical contrast or a useful formally proved theorem. |
| **Social ontology already treats joint facts** | Gilbert's *Joint Commitment* and Bratman's *Shared Agency* analyze genuinely joint activities, commitments and intentions. | Draft.2 adds §§4.32–4.33 as clear illustrations, while naming the predecessors. Do not call them original discoveries. |
| **The other-minds account is philosophically contestable** | Direct perception, expression and second-person approaches dispute any assumption that knowing another person is only indirect private inference. | Draft.2 §1.13 allows direct understanding without claiming infallibility. Bayesian beliefs remain a model for selected uncertain claims, not a complete theory of knowledge. |
| **The total-variation identity is standard** | The new Lean module proves a common-denominator binary Bayes testing identity, not a novel mathematical insight. | Label the result an exact checked illustration, not a research breakthrough. Require a more discriminating dynamic result before publishing as formal methods. |
| **The consent-conditioned experiment is stipulated** | An exogenously fixed approval bit cannot reveal preferences, although actual approvals might be model-dependent and informative. | Explicitly restrict the theorem's scope. In the genuine dynamic problem, treat approvals and withdrawals as observations and permission constraints without inferring every hidden motive. |
| **The adaptive coupling bound is standard and not Lean-checked** | The finite-horizon TV inequality is a textbook-type union/coupling argument, with a demanding conditional kernel bound. | Publish it as a traditional, conditional lemma only. Any later Lean claim requires a separate checked source, not a citation to this note. |
| **Creation and revelation need not compete metaphysically** | Stable propensities can coexist with newly realized agreements; apparent state change can arise from altered reporting. | Compare restricted *observable* mechanisms and interpret stable propensity versus actual joint fact as distinct targets. Abandon a simple R-versus-C metaphysical dichotomy. |
| **Randomization of an offer does not identify treatment mechanisms** | Uptake is selected by both agents; the offer itself may influence expectations, and post-treatment missingness is not random. | Specify ITT, reporting/invariance measures, missingness sensitivity, and independent transfer outcomes. No causal mediation inference from a single report. |
| **The outcome may be just individual learning** | Better joint-task scores can follow from skill gain or shared instruction, not dyad-specific constitution. | Separate independent proficiency, transfer, blinded scoring, and partner specificity in a later validated design. The proposed pilot is not yet confirmatory. |
| **The Tractatus may still be verbose** | Even 78 numbered propositions may sound like familiar insights wearing a prestigious title and formal architecture. | Preserve one strong argument and cut lines that fail to advance it after independent literary/philosophical review. Numbering is not evidence of deductive entailment. |

## Minimum defensible scientific contribution

A research paper should make *at least one* contribution genuinely beyond the established premises and elementary identities. Possible routes:

1. An **identification theorem** for two agents with private reports, history-dependent relational transitions and independently restrictable permissible actions. State clear necessary/sufficient assumptions and give a genuine counterexample to weaker assumptions.
2. A **registered empirical design** that, through credible measures, distinguishes action-induced dyadic change from the strongest plausible stable-propensity/reporting alternatives; include interdependence, dropout and external validation.
3. A **philosophical argument** showing why the practical sufficiency of knowledge and joint permissions cannot be reduced to a single-agent information-maximization problem, while engaging Bratman, Gilbert, direct-perception accounts and transformative choice. This may be valuable as philosophy even without a novel statistic.

The current programme has beginnings of all three but has completed none.

## Recommended next release gates

- **Do not add more notation simply to increase apparent depth.** Inspect whether every formal result proves something *not obvious from its own definitions*.
- A prospective theorem must include at least one meaningful adversarial countermodel, explicit assumptions, formal proof/CI evidence when claimed, and comparison to Blackwell/adaptive experiment design/causal inference.
- Build a systematic literature-search protocol and primary-source reading ledger instead of treating a targeted web search as exhaustive.
- Commission readers from philosophy of mind, social ontology, formal epistemology, causal inference and relationship science; disclose that their feedback is independent only when it actually occurs.
- Do not translate or certify a final Latin/English edition until the Indonesian philosophical source is stable.
- Publish measured negative findings and unresolved identifiability honestly, not as proof of an unfalsifiable theory.

**Editorial judgment:** continue. But the right next step is to expose a weakness that might defeat the proposed contribution, then repair it if possible. A work becomes more valuable when it is precise about what would make it wrong.
