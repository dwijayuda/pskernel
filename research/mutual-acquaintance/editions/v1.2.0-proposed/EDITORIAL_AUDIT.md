## Consistency risks

1. **Showing ≠ reliable identification.** §6.96 deliberately says observed acts do not necessarily fix all intentions or causes, consistent with §6.82, §6.93 and observational non-identification.
2. **Words are evidence too.** §6.95 does not dismiss reports; the proposition explicitly includes them within possible knowledge.
3. **Avoid a category error with Wittgenstein.** The early Wittgenstein's logical `zeigen` is not interchangeable with observing interpersonal behavior. This literary application is an analogy.
4. **Avoid skeptical absolutism.** §7 prohibits claims *as though already known*, not provisional conjecture, probabilistic inference, or acknowledging uncertainty. Read its epistemic norm in light of earlier probabilistic passages.
5. **Versioning and translations.** Neither v1.1.0 nor old companions are silently rewritten. EN and LA are provisional translations; LA requires philological review.
6. **Originality.** This edit strengthens the ending's epistemological discipline but makes no empirical psychological discovery and proves no new theorem.

## Verification boundary

`check_proposed_edition.py` checks labels, exact verbatim preservation for 115 unchanged propositions, the approved Indonesian wording and shared order across translations. It does not establish philosophical truth, literary adequacy, translation accuracy, or theorem verification.
