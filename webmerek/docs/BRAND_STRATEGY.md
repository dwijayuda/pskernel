# WebMerek brand strategy and identity guide

Proposed identity, 25 October 2026. Original implementation; no affiliation with Flourish Online. Name/domain/trademark clearance remains unresolved [L2, L2b].

## Purpose and positioning

Mission: help good service businesses explain their value clearly through strategy, identity and a useful website. Vision: become a dependable specialist for expert-led Indonesian service businesses, judged by clarity and delivery rather than inflated claims.

Positioning hypothesis: for consultants and professional training businesses whose capability is ahead of their presentation, WebMerek combines a focused strategic brief, coherent identity and bounded website execution, with transparent scope and final asset handover.

## Message options considered

| Candidate | Strength | Reason not selected as the primary line |
|---|---|---|
| Branding dan website profesional untuk bisnis yang ingin tampil lebih meyakinkan | Explicit service description | Long and generic as a memorable brand line; useful explanatory copy instead |
| Dari bagus menjadi dipercaya | Connects appearance and trust | Risks implying design alone establishes trust |
| Biar kualitasnya terlihat. | Short, natural, tied to an existing-business problem | **Selected**, accompanied by an explicit service description |

Primary hero: **Bisnisnya sudah bagus. Saatnya terlihat.** Supporting copy explains the service and intended audience. It does not guarantee customers, ranking, growth or business legitimacy.

## Personality and voice

Calm, clear, candid, capable, warm without forced slang. A guide/craftsperson metaphor may help internal consistency but is not a psychological diagnosis or scientific customer segmentation.

Write natural Bahasa Indonesia: “lingkup”, “proses”, “file final”, “siap ditinjau”. Explain necessary technical terms such as static HTML and CMS. Prefer concrete limits over “solusi 360”, “revolusioner”, “unlimited”, “pasti laris”, or “nomor satu”. Do not address every prospect as a beginner or assume gender, religion, age or city.

Example: “Website statis hingga lima halaman, dengan dua putaran revisi dan panduan serah terima.” Not: “Website premium tanpa batas yang dijamin menaikkan omzet.”

## Visual system

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | `#F5F2EB` | Main warm neutral surface |
| White | `#FFFEFA` | Content contrast |
| Ink | `#202922` | Primary text and dark sections |
| Muted | `#586057` | Secondary readable text |
| Blue | `#2444C4` | Main actions and distinctive emphasis |
| Mint | `#D4E3D2` | Reflective concept surfaces |
| Orange | `#F47B4F` | Decorative accent, not small body text |

System Arial/Helvetica provides direct readable body copy; Georgia supplies an editorial italic contrast. No external font service or font license dependency is introduced. The small original SVG mark uses a simplified W-like geometry; the wordmark is `webmerek.` in the interface while business prose uses **WebMerek**.

Layout: generous whitespace, left-aligned editorial headlines, disciplined borders, a restrained card system, and real visual hierarchy rather than a wall of rounded boxes. Three offers appear together on larger screens and become readable stacked units on mobile. Motion is limited to small interaction transitions and respects reduced-motion preferences.

## Original concept work

“Ruang Reka” and “Tumbuh Akal” are fictional explorations created for this website. Labels appear on preview cards and detailed pages. Similar real names, if any, do not imply affiliation. These examples demonstrate a visual thought process, not completed client work, legal mark availability, or measured business outcomes. Replace or supplement them with real case evidence only after client permission.

No Flourish imagery, logo, testimonials, proprietary wording or distinctive page composition is reused. The site's visual language is independent; the reference is used for business-model analysis.

## Brand governance

Keep pricing in `site.json`; keep terminology consistent with the service documents. Do not invent proof to fill empty space. Maintain explicit concept and preview labels until appropriate conditions change. Use actual studio availability only after confirming staffing. All photography, client marks, paid fonts and stock assets introduced later need documented rights.

Before launch, review spelling and comprehension with five target buyers as a qualitative exercise. Ask them to explain what is offered, for whom, at what approximate cost, and what happens next. Five opinions are not market validation but can expose unclear language.
