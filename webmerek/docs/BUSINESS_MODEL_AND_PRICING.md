# Business model and pricing hypotheses

Version: 25 October 2026. Prices are **proposed**, not observed willingness to pay or confirmed commercial offers. Source of truth for site prices and arithmetic: `../site.json`. Competitor observations are separately recorded in [COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md](COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md).

These are one-time service fees except optional care. Domain, hosting, paid licenses, applicable taxes, travel, complex illustration, naming, trademark filing, additional pages/languages and new functionality are excluded unless a written quote adds them. No payment is processed on this site.

## Why these boundaries

The offer must be understandable and deliverable before it is scalable. A five-page static site is not a CMS, store, booking platform, application or integration service. The package fits low-frequency updates and source handover. A buyer needing daily nontechnical editing should receive a separate CMS assessment or referral—not a hidden workaround.

The combined price is less than buying the two parts separately because shared strategy and coordinated production are assumed to reduce duplicate effort. That efficiency is an assumption requiring tracked pilot hours, not a reason to discount identical effort indefinitely.

## Proposed unit economics

Planning direct labor: **Rp90,000 per delivery hour**, a blended fully loaded assumption including founder labor. Other direct costs represent allocated project-specific QA/assets/tools; paid client-specific purchases beyond allowance require approval.

| Website | 32 | Rp2,880,000 | Rp320,000 | Rp3,200,000 | Rp4,700,000 / 59.49% |

Gross contribution is not take-home pay. It must fund selling, nondelivery management, administration, shared tools, rework reserves and tax. Do not count the same founder delivery hours again inside overhead.

## Billing and working capital

Propose 50% start / 50% final approval for separate packages, and 50% start / 30% identity approval / 20% final approval for the combined package. Confirm milestones and acceptance in a signed scope. An advance is a cash receipt, not automatically earned revenue. Do not spend deposits as though all remaining delivery liabilities disappeared.

Domain/hosting accounts should be registered to the client. Transfer final original files after agreed payment; third-party licenses remain governed by their owners. Do not copy Flourish's six-month financing structure before WebMerek can assess credit and cash risks.

## Revision and acceptance rules

A round means one consolidated feedback list from the appointed decision maker on the submitted phase. Corrections to agreed deliverables are distinct from new scope. A new direction after approval, extra page, integration or changed audience needs a written change order with time/cost impact. No unlimited revisions.

The clock starts after signed scope, agreed payment, inputs and access are ready. Record waiting time separately from production. The 30-day correction period addresses implementation defects against the accepted scope, not new content, ongoing hosting failures or new features.

## Price validation experiment

Present the same explicit scope and price to eligible buyers, record acceptance and reasons for rejection, and avoid fabricated “normal prices” or unearned scarcity. Three pilot wins would support continued testing, not establish national demand. Track real time by discovery, design, development, QA and revisions. If work requires more hours, change the product or price rather than hiding unpaid labor.

Review tax treatment before issuing a real invoice [L3, L3b]; neither these estimates nor the site assumes a universal VAT/withholding regime. Retain quoted gross, actual cash received, any valid withholding evidence and accounting revenue as separate fields.
