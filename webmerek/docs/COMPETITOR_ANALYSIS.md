## What this comparison does not prove

It does not rank service quality, measure customer satisfaction, establish average selling prices, or identify market shares. Search position is not business success. No provider's absence of extracted prices means it never publishes prices. No annual total is fabricated from a monthly headline when contract/setup conditions remain unclear.

## Competitive choice

**Proposal:** specialize in owner-led expert service businesses that need positioning, coherent identity and a small information website, with a transparent scope and client-owned final assets. The niche must be validated. “We do branding and websites” is not a defensible distinction because several observed competitors do both.

The alternative set also includes doing nothing, improving an existing profile, using a site builder, hiring a freelancer, or keeping a simple brochure site. A buyer satisfied with those choices is not automatically an uneducated prospect.

## Matched-quote checklist for validation

When a prospect voluntarily compares providers, record the same page count, content authorship/word limit, unique versus template design, identity scope, revisions, hosting period, renewal, ownership, editor/CMS, accessibility testing, maintenance, taxes and response commitments. Obtain permission and remove personal or confidential information. Evaluate first-year cash requirement and recurring obligations separately from the value of strategic work.

## Implications for WebMerek

1. Publish enough scope to prevent “unlimited design” expectations, including the static-site limitation.
2. Make preparation and handover visible; they are usable operational commitments, unlike unsupported sales guarantees.
3. Demonstrate original thinking through labeled concepts now; replace with genuine, permissioned cases only after delivery.
4. Use actual project hours and paid acceptance to validate price. Competitor price observations alone cannot validate Rp4.9m/Rp7.9m/Rp11.9m.
