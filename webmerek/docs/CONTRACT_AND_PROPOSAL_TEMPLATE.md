# Proposal and service-agreement drafting template

**Draft for qualified Indonesian legal/tax review, not legal advice, not an executed or legally validated contract.** Do not send this as a final agreement until operator details, governing arrangements and consumer/data obligations have been assessed [L1–L3]. Brackets are intentional unresolved fields and never appear as fake credentials on the public site.

## Proposal cover

Project: [client business / actual project]. Operator: [verified legal name and address]. Client: [verified contracting party]. Date and validity: [agreed dates]. Decision maker: [authorized person].

**Problem in the client's words:** [confirmed need]. **Objective:** [measurable deliverable or comprehension outcome, not guaranteed sales]. **Recommended package:** [one selected offer]. **Why this fits:** [requirements and boundaries].

## Statement of work schedule

## Commercial terms to review

State the total service fee, separately approved expenses and applicable tax treatment after review. Proposed billing is 50/50 for separate packages or 50/30/20 for the combined scope. Specify invoice due dates, lawful withholding documentation and late-payment handling with legal review. Do not insert a penalty or blanket “all deposits nonrefundable” term without considering work earned, committed costs and applicable obligations.

A change order must state the requested change, added/removed deliverables, price, schedule and authorization. Work outside scope begins only after written agreement.

## Rights and materials

The client confirms authority to supply materials and approves publication rights. Define transfer of final original work after agreed payment, reserving pre-existing reusable tools and respecting third-party licenses. Define whether editable source is included; do not promise formats the team cannot produce. Domain and hosting accounts are client-owned. Portfolio use and testimonials require a separate optional permission, not assumed consent.

## Confidentiality and data

Identify confidential material, permitted recipients, approved tools, minimum-access practices, retention/deletion and incident reporting. Where personal data is processed, document the parties' roles, purposes, lawful basis, processors and cross-border considerations as applicable. Do not put credentials or customer data in this public GitHub repository. Client approval of AI-assisted workflows does not eliminate the operator's obligations.

## Cancellation, defects and disputes

Agree a fair process for suspension, termination, handover of paid work, accounting for completed work/committed costs and refund of unearned balances where applicable. Define a 30-day in-scope defect-correction window separately from feature changes and hosting-provider incidents. Review liability language and any exclusions rather than relying on a generic blanket waiver. Fill governing law, jurisdiction/dispute steps and notices with professional advice and both parties' agreement.

## Acceptance record

Client confirms the agreed checklist has been reviewed; unresolved items are listed with an owner and resolution date. A signature is not permission to publish confidential work or marketing claims unless separately agreed. Record final files and access handed over, payment status, support dates and the named contact for issues.
