# Customer acquisition and sales operating system

Proposal, not an active campaign. No prospects have been contacted and no personal data has been collected through this assignment. Sources and channel cautions: [M1, M2], [L1], competitor ledger [C1–C10].

## Initial funnel

Educational article or permissioned referral → clear offer page → self-check or short brief → human fit conversation → matched written scope → signed agreement and deposit → delivery → permissioned case/referral.

The current site stops at a local brief download. Activating real contact requires verified contact/identity, privacy review and owner authorization. Do not interpret this preview as a live lead-collection system.

## First 90-day acquisition hypothesis

Recruit 60 well-matched prospects through existing relationships, professional communities and individually researched business contacts. Proposed directional funnel: 15 conversations, nine qualified opportunities, six proposals, three paid projects. These are planning targets, not measured conversion rates. Three wins from Rp3m of attributable cash acquisition spend would mean Rp1m cash CAC; fully loaded CAC must also include sales labor and other attributable cost.

A separate 20-person research interview cohort may overlap only with permission; interviews are not disguised sales commitments. Capture objections from nonbuyers, not just praise from friends.

## Channels and decisions

- **Warm referrals and partners first:** ask suitable collaborators for introductions with consent. Potential partners include writers, photographers and complementary service providers. No paid referral relationship is assumed or authorized.
- **Professional communities and LinkedIn:** test clear examples for consultants/training businesses. Avoid mass unsolicited DMs or scraping personal contact data.
- **Website and Instagram:** publish actual process and concept-label transparency. Organic content is a long-term experiment, not a promise of rankings or instant leads.
- **Paid acquisition later:** only after two delivered pilots and a functioning, legally reviewed contact path. Start with a capped experiment and stop if qualified-inquiry cost exceeds the contribution economics. Do not spend advertising money in this assignment.

TikTok, marketplace distribution and Google Business Profile are optional experiments. Verify eligibility before using business-profile features or displaying a physical address.

## Outreach examples in Bahasa Indonesia

### Permission-first introduction

> Halo [nama], saya [nama operator terverifikasi] dari WebMerek. Saya melihat [pengamatan spesifik yang benar] pada penawaran [bisnis]. Kami sedang mengembangkan layanan untuk membantu bisnis jasa memperjelas pesan dan website. Apakah berkenan kalau saya mengirim dua catatan singkat tentang kejelasan halaman layanan Anda? Tidak perlu membalas bila belum relevan.

Only use this after operator identity and permission to trade are established. Replace brackets with verified information; never use a fabricated personalization.

### After permission

> Terima kasih. Dari halaman publik yang saya lihat, [hal yang sudah jelas] sudah membantu. Dua hal yang mungkin bisa diperjelas adalah [observasi] dan [observasi]. Ini tinjauan awal, bukan penilaian menyeluruh. Bila menjadi prioritas, kita bisa membahas kebutuhan dan kecocokan lingkupnya terlebih dahulu.

### One follow-up, then stop

> Halo [nama], saya menindaklanjuti catatan yang sebelumnya Anda izinkan. Apakah kebutuhan ini masih menjadi prioritas? Bila belum, saya tidak akan melanjutkan follow-up untuk saat ini.

Do not automatically add the contact to marketing lists. Record opt-outs and respect platform rules.

## Discovery agenda, 25 minutes proposed

Five minutes: business, buyer and trigger. Ten minutes: current materials, what is misunderstood, previous attempts and evidence. Five minutes: budget, timing, decision maker and content/CMS/integration requirements. Five minutes: summarize fit, exclusions and next step. Never manufacture urgency or promise a result outside the product.

Ask: “Apa yang baru-baru ini membuat Anda merasa perlu mengubah merek atau website?” and “Bagian apa yang harus bisa Anda perbarui sendiri?” Record actual answers, not assumed needs.

## Qualification rubric

A qualified opportunity has a real service offering, a specific business problem, authorized decision maker, attainable budget, required material rights, and requirements fitting the package. Unknown items are follow-up questions, not automatic high scores. Reject or refer complex applications, daily CMS needs without a suitable product, or guaranteed-growth expectations.

## Proposal structure

One-page decision summary; current problem in the client's words; objective; exact deliverables/page map/word allowance; exclusions; client responsibilities; timeline and readiness; two revision rounds; fees/taxes/expenses; payment milestones; change-order rule; ownership/licenses; acceptance; support; privacy and cancellation process. Include the verified legal operator details only after review. See [contract template](CONTRACT_AND_PROPOSAL_TEMPLATE.md).

## CRM fields and metrics

Use a minimal access-controlled register after privacy review: business, permission source/date, need, package, stage, next agreed contact date, quoted fee, loss reason, deposit, earned revenue and actual hours. Avoid personal sensitive data. Define retention and deletion; do not keep data forever by default.

Measure separately: page visits (only if lawful measurement is enabled), button clicks, actual messages, qualified calls, proposals, signed agreements, collected deposits, recognized revenue, direct costs, revisions and referrals. A WhatsApp link click does not prove a sent message, and a deposit is not automatically profit.

## Retention

Offer capped care only when a recurring update need exists. At handover explain self-management and other-provider options. Ask for a factual review or case study only after delivery, with permission and the client's approval of claims. A referral request must be optional and must not imply an undisclosed incentive.
