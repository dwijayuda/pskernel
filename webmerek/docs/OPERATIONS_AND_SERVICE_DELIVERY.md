# Operations and delivery system

Proposed operating system. Named personnel, employment status and current capacity are not established by this repository. Do not sell availability until the operator has staffed it.

## Roles and capacity

Business owner/account lead owns qualification, scope and billing. Brand strategist/designer owns positioning and visual direction. Frontend implementer owns static delivery. A reviewer owns copy, links, accessibility checks and acceptance evidence. A person may fill multiple roles, but approval and quality checks must still be explicit.

Planning capacity: 160 delivery hours/month for the assembled team, with nondelivery time separately budgeted. Start with at most two simultaneous production projects. The optimistic financial case needs 224 hours of available delivery capacity and additional contractor support; it is not free capacity from the same people.

## Workflow and gates

| Phase | Inputs / work | Exit evidence | Proposed ownership |
|---|---|---|---|
| Qualification | Need, buyer, budget, CMS/integration fit | Written fit decision, no unsupported promise | Account lead |
| Scope | Page list, words, revisions, exclusions, fees | Approved scope and legally reviewed agreement | Owner + client decision maker |
| Readiness | Materials, rights, access, deposit, review dates | Checklist signed off; production clock begins | Account lead |
| Strategy | 90-minute session and positioning summary | Agreed primary audience and message | Strategist |
| Direction | One visual direction and content architecture | Consolidated client approval | Designer |
| Production | Identity assets and/or static pages | Reviewable version against scope | Designer/developer |
| Review | Two consolidated revision rounds | Logged decisions and resolved in-scope comments | One client decision maker |
| QA | Content, mobile, keyboard, links, metadata, contact | Acceptance checklist with known limitations | Reviewer |
| Handover | Final approval/payment, files and access | Handover inventory and client acknowledgment | Account lead |
| Correction/care | In-scope defect window, optional care separately | Ticket resolution and updated records | Delivery owner |

Production estimates exclude waiting for materials or approvals. Agree review windows in writing; proposed default is three working days per review. Missing inputs move the schedule transparently rather than silently consuming capacity. Do not unilaterally apply unspecified penalties.

## Input checklist

Business description, actual services/pricing, target buyer, differentiator hypotheses, existing mark files, proof and permissions, photography rights, legal copy owner, page list, decision maker, expected update frequency, actual contact details and domain/hosting access. Never ask a client to send passwords in a public issue or shared brief. Use approved access-sharing methods outside this public repository.

## Revision and change management

Collect feedback in one numbered list with page/component references. Separate errors against approved work from preference changes and new scope. A change order records request, reason, deliverables, hours/fee, schedule impact and written authorization. Work does not begin on an unapproved extra. Keep past approvals so neither party must rely on memory.

## Quality definition

Content accurately describes the client; proof is genuine and permitted; navigation and contact work; layouts do not overflow on target viewports; keyboard focus and labels are checked; media has appropriate alternatives; metadata uses the actual domain; no preview/noindex flags accidentally remain on a cleared launch; there are no exposed secrets or test contacts. Automated accessibility scans supplement, not replace, human review [T2, T3].

Static HTML is a deliberate product boundary. Do not install a hidden CMS or external form processor to make a sales promise appear fulfilled. Additional infrastructure requires scope, privacy, cost and support review.

## Handover inventory

Original final SVG/PNG identity files, brand guide, agreed editable template formats, HTML/CSS/JS sources, approved assets and license notes, deployment instructions, verified account ownership, content-update guide, known limitations, acceptance record and the correction-window dates. Noneditable proprietary formats must not be promised unless actually delivered.

The client owns domain/hosting accounts. Final original work transfers according to the reviewed agreement after payment; pre-existing tools and third-party rights remain distinct. No forced maintenance subscription. Document a path to another maintainer.

## Support policy hypothesis

Thirty days of correction for defects against accepted scope. Response targets need actual staffing; no 24/7 claim. Optional care provides up to two hours monthly of minor updates and link checks, without rollover. New pages, rebranding, outages at a hosting provider, incident response and new integrations are not silently included. Provide a separate quote or referral.

## AI and data handling

AI may assist drafts and code under human review. Do not upload client confidential/personal data to a third-party model without an approved basis, contract and suitable settings. Verify originality, claims, licenses and technical output. No AI-generated fake staff, clients, reviews or performance metrics. Public repositories store only public project material, never production customer records.

## Operational metrics

Track hours per phase, calendar waiting time, rework causes, revision consumption, gross contribution including founder delivery time, on-time handover against readiness-adjusted commitments, and acceptance issues. Review after every pilot. If two projects exceed estimated hours by more than a proposed 20%, suspend scaling until scope/price/process is corrected.
