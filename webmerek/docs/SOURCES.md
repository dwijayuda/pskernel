# Sources and evidence ledger

Research access date: **25 October 2026**. Public pages may subsequently change. Facts describe the material visible at access, not an assurance of current availability at purchase. No contact form, services-book opt-in, competitor inquiry, or paid transaction was submitted. No private business metrics were accessed.

## Evidence convention

**Observed:** public page or official report states it. **Inference:** our interpretation, not a source claim. **Proposal:** a WebMerek decision to test. **Unknown:** not established. Advertised prices are not evidence of sales, quality, willingness to pay, or final tax-inclusive cost. Business-source claims are first-party marketing, not independent audit results. Primary official technical documents were used for implementation choices.
